Compounding is what happens when your returns begin earning returns of their own. The effect is quiet at first, then increasingly visible as the years pass.

## Time does the heavy lifting

Two investors can contribute the same amount and end with very different results if one starts earlier. Each contribution gets more time in the market, so the later growth builds on a larger base.

Try different monthly contributions, time periods, and expected returns in the [SIP calculator](../calculators/sip.html). The result is an estimate, but it makes the trade-off between time and contribution easier to see.
